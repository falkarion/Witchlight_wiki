These turned up overnight, pinned to every door, fence post and tavern board for miles around. Nobody saw who put them there.

![[00-012.witchlight-carnival-ticket.png]]

> [!flyer] Mister Witch & Mister Light present
> ## The Witchlight Carnival
> *Returned at last, after eight long years!*
>
> **Marvels from a hundred worlds, gathered in a single field.**
>
> Thrill to the acrobats of the **Big Top**! Ride the **Carousel** that never stops! Lose yourself in the **Hall of Illusions**, glide across the lake on the **Gondola Swans**, and cheer on the fastest snails you'll ever see!
>
> Feast in the **Feasting Orchard**, try your luck at a hundred games of skill, and carry home **prizes** you won't find anywhere else.
>
> **And in the last hour before dawn, one lucky guest will be crowned the Witchlight Monarch!**
>
> **Gates open at dusk. Gates close before dawn.**
>
> *Admission: grown folk, 8 silver. Little ones, 3 silver.*
>
> *Short of coin? Our ticketer loves a fair trade.*

## On the back of every ticket
> [!flyer] Three little rules keep the Witchlight bright
> 1. Every gift is owed a gift.
> 2. Be a good guest, and we'll be a good host.
> 3. What isn't yours stays where it is.
