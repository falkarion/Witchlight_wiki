---
title: "What People Say"
---
When the flyers go up, everyone suddenly has a story about the Witchlight Carnival. Some of these are true. Some are half true. Some are what happens when a tale is told by a grandmother, then a tavern, then a very small cousin.

> [!rumour] "It comes every eight years, regular as a comet. Nobody has ever seen it arrive. One evening the field is empty, and the next there are tents."

> [!rumour] "The owners are two elves, Witch and Light. My father saw them when he was a boy. He swears they haven't aged a day."

> [!rumour] "Buy a ticket, don't sneak in. My gran says it's bad manners. My gran says a lot of things."

> [!rumour] "Whatever you've lost, you'll find it at the Witchlight, if you know where to look."

> [!rumour] "The calliope plays all night long, and there's never anyone sitting at the keys."

> [!rumour] "Old Tam says there's a tent in there that doesn't lead back out, only somewhere else entirely. Old Tam also says he once married a mermaid."

> [!rumour] "The snail races are rigged. The fastest snail is a wizard. Everybody knows that."

> [!rumour] "The candyfloss is made from real clouds. That's why it never rains while the carnival's in town."

> [!rumour] "Half the folk who work there aren't from this world. That's where the strange ones come from: other worlds, other skies."

> [!rumour] "Don't laugh at the clowns. Or do laugh at the clowns. I can never remember which one gets you into trouble."

> [!rumour] "Never tell the carnival folk your real name. My aunt told one of them hers was Duchess Marigold of the Seven Hills. It's Brenda."

> [!rumour] "If one of the carnival folk gives you something, give something back. Doesn't matter what. Uncle Bert gave one a sock, and he's been lucky ever since."

> [!rumour] "Be polite in there. Rude guests get turned into things. Toads, mostly."

> [!rumour] "Don't eat anything at the carnival. Except the cake. No, wait: especially not the cake."

> [!rumour] "Cousin Wim went to the carnival for one night and came home a week late. That's his story, anyway. His wife has her own."

> [!rumour] "Don't still be inside when the sun comes up. Or you'll leave with it."

*Which of these are true? You'll have to go and see.*
