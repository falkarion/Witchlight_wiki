*A fairy tale with teeth.*

Once every eight years, a carnival comes to town. Nobody sees it arrive. One evening there are empty fields at the edge of the woods, and the next there are tents striped in every color, lanterns strung between the trees, and music drifting out of a calliope that never seems to need anyone to play it. Its gates open at dusk and close before dawn, and after a few nights it's gone again.

They call it the **Witchlight Carnival**. People say it has travelled everywhere: to every kingdom, every world, places no map has heard of. People say you can find anything there, if you know where to look. People say a lot of things.

This campaign begins on the night the carnival returns.

## What to expect
- **Whimsy on top, loss and bargains underneath.** Talking animals, snail races and sugar cupcakes, and under all of it, old promises, stolen things and people who don't play fair.
- **Cleverness counts.** Many problems can be solved by talking, trading, tricking or understanding them. Fighting is always an option; it isn't always the best one.
- **You arrive separately.** Each of your characters comes to the carnival for their own reasons, and none of you know each other yet. How you meet is part of the story.
- **Your stories matter.** Each of your characters' pasts is woven into what happens.
- **We play D&D 5e (2014 rules).** See [[House Rules]].
