---
## The rules we play by
- **D&D 5e, 2014 rules.** We don't use the 2024 rules: no weapon mastery, no origin feats at 1st level, no Heroic Inspiration (regular inspiration works as in the 2014 Player's Handbook).
- **D&D Beyond** sometimes mixes 2024 material into a 2014 character. If your sheet shows weapon mastery properties or 2024 actions, ignore them.
- **Levels:** you start at **1st level**. We use milestone levelling: you level up at important moments in the story, not by counting experience points.

## Building a character
- **Ability scores:** point buy (27 points).
- **Hit points:** maximum at 1st level. Each level after that, gain the fixed average for your class (d6: 4, d8: 5, d10: 6, d12: 7) plus your Constitution modifier. On D&D Beyond, set Hit Point Type to *Fixed*.
- **Starting equipment:** take the starting equipment from your class and your background, not the starting-gold option.
- **Books we use:** the Player's Handbook, Dungeon Master's Guide and Monster Manual (2014), and *The Wild Beyond the Witchlight*.
- **From *The Wild Beyond the Witchlight*:** the fairy and harengon races, and the Feylost and Witchlight Hand backgrounds. You'll find them on [D&D Beyond](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/twbtw/character-options).
  - **Fairy:** at our table, fairies also have **Soft Landing**: you have resistance to falling damage.
- **Custom backgrounds** are fine, following the rules in the Player's Handbook: two skills, two tool or language proficiencies, and matching starting equipment. Talk to the DM.
- **Other books and homebrew:** options from other books, or homebrew, may be possible. Ask the DM.

## At the table
- **Talk to the DM** about anything you'd rather not see in the game, or would like to see more of.
- **Your character's secrets** stay between you and the DM. What other players learn about your character, they learn in play.
